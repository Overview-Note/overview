# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Overview to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, identity, or affiliation. We pledge to
make participation a positive experience for all.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in speech and action.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Assume good intent; ask questions before assuming the worst.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Discrimination or exclusionary language of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and to
anyone representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may
remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, and contributions that violate this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior they deem
inappropriate.

Report concerns to the maintainers via the contact in the repository profile. Reports
will be reviewed and handled confidentially.
